About QuickBite

A neighbourhood kitchen that believes good food should not take long to reach you.

Who we are

QuickBite is a home-style restaurant and delivery kitchen. We cook pizzas, burgers, North Indian curries and Indo-Chinese dishes, and we make each one fresh after you order it.

Our menu is short on purpose. We would rather cook 19 dishes well than 100 dishes in a hurry.

Our mission

To deliver tasty, freshly cooked food at fair prices, on time, with a smile from the person at the door.

Our vision

To become the first place people in the neighbourhood think of when they are hungry, and to do it without ever cutting corners on quality.

Our story

QuickBite began as a small kitchen run by a family who wanted to serve the kind of food they cooked at home. Word spread through the neighbourhood, and the orders kept growing.

  • 2021Opened a 3-table kitchen with a menu of 8 dishes.
  • 2023Started home delivery with two riders.
  • 2025Added pizzas, burgers and a full drinks menu.
  • 2026Launched online ordering so you can order in a few taps.
